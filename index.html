THE CHALLENGE

A matric certificate alone doesn't open doors.

Limpopo's matriculants are passing in growing numbers, but passing is not the same as being seen. Achievements go unrecorded, records are scattered across schools, and employers, universities and bursary providers have no reliable way to verify what a learner has actually accomplished.

🎯

Our Mission

To give every Limpopo learner a single, verified digital portfolio that follows them from Grade 11 through to employment or higher education - built once, trusted everywhere.

🌍

Our Vision

A province where a learner's potential is never lost to paperwork - where teachers, districts, universities and employers can act on the same trusted record.

🔒

Our Approach

Learners build it. Teachers verify it. Districts confirm it against official records under strict privacy protocols. Only then can bursaries and universities access it.

THE LEARNER'S JOURNEY

From Grade 11 to what comes next.

BrightPath is built around the two years that matter most - and the transition immediately after them.

01

Grade 11

Learner registers, builds a profile and starts logging achievements - academic, sport, leadership, technology and community work - as they happen.

02

Grade 12

Teachers verify achievements throughout the year. The district matches the portfolio against its own learner records and approves it for external access.

03

Academia

Universities and colleges, once granted district approval, view the verified portfolio alongside the matric certificate when considering the learner.

04

Employment

The learner downloads their verified portfolio as a referral letter - a credible, district-endorsed introduction to bursary providers and employers.

Why Limpopo needs this

Limpopo's matric pass rate rose to 86.1% in 2025, up from 85.01% in 2024, with all ten districts now passing above 80% - a real and growing achievement for the province's largely rural school system. Yet that success has not translated into equal opportunity: youth unemployment (ages 15-34) in Limpopo sits at roughly 45.5%, well above the national average of 32.9%, and access to further study or work remains hardest for learners furthest from urban centres. BrightPath exists to close that gap - by making a learner's verified record travel with them, past the school gate, into the hands of the people who can act on it.

Figures: Limpopo Department of Basic Education NSC results (2024/2025); Statistics South Africa, Quarterly Labour Force Survey (Q1 2024). Provided as context, not a live data feed.
