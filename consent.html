BEFORE YOU REGISTER

Consent, Terms & Rights

BrightPath handles personal information belonging to learners, in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). Read the terms for your role before creating an account.

Who this covers

The learner registering, and where the learner is a minor, their parent or legal guardian who consents on their behalf.

What we collect

  • Name, grade, school, district and a contact detail.
  • Academic marks entered by the learner.
  • Achievements and any supporting evidence links the learner submits.
  • Verification status and comments added by teachers and the district.

How it is used

To build a verifiable digital portfolio that the learner controls, that teachers can verify, and that - only once the district approves it - bursary providers and universities may view.

Your rights

  • You may view, correct or delete your information at any time.
  • Your portfolio is never visible to a bursary or university until your district has approved it.
  • You decide what achievements to submit; you may withdraw consent and request account deletion.
  • Where you are under 18, a parent or guardian must consent to registration and processing of your information, as required by POPIA section 35.

Consent

By registering, the learner (and parent/guardian, where the learner is a minor) consents to BrightPath processing this information for the purposes above, and to it being shared with the learner's school, district and any bursary/university the district approves.

Who this covers

Teaching staff verifying learner achievements on behalf of a registered school.

What we collect

  • Your name, school and role at the school.
  • A record of which achievements you verified, rejected, or commented on, and when.

How it is used

To attach your verification decisions to learner achievements, and to give the school and district an auditable record of who confirmed what.

Your rights and responsibilities

  • You may only verify achievements for learners at your registered school.
  • You must have a reasonable basis (evidence, personal knowledge, or a certificate) before marking an achievement verified.
  • Your verification actions are logged and may be reviewed by the district.
  • You may request correction of your own account details at any time.

Consent

By registering, you consent to BrightPath recording your verification actions against your name and school, and to your school and district being able to view that verification history.

Who this covers

Officials from a Limpopo education district office responsible for verifying and approving learner portfolios.

What we collect

  • Your name, district office and official capacity.
  • A record of match checks and approval/rejection decisions you make on learner records.

Protected policies and protocols

  • Learner records may only be matched against your district's own official learner records - not another district's.
  • Approval for bursary/university access must only be granted once a match is confirmed and at least one achievement is verified.
  • All approval and match decisions are logged with your account and timestamp for audit purposes, in line with the Department of Basic Education's data governance policy and POPIA's accountability principle.
  • District accounts must not share login credentials between officials.

Your rights and responsibilities

  • You may revoke a previously granted bursary/university approval at any time.
  • You are accountable for approvals made under your account.

Consent

By registering, you confirm you are authorised by your district office to perform these functions, and consent to your actions being logged against your account.

Who this covers

Registered bursary providers, employers and universities/colleges seeking to view verified learner portfolios.

What you may access

  • Only portfolios that a district has explicitly approved for external access.
  • Only achievements marked "Verified" - pending or rejected achievements are never shown to your account.
  • Only Grade 12 learners may issue you a downloadable referral letter.

What we collect about you

  • Your organisation's name and registration details, and a contact person.
  • A log of which learner portfolios your account has requested or viewed.

Your obligations

  • Learner information accessed through BrightPath may only be used for the stated purpose (bursary, scholarship or admissions consideration) and may not be sold, shared with third parties, or used for marketing.
  • You must not attempt to contact a learner using information obtained here outside of the stated application/admissions process.
  • Access can be suspended by a district or by BrightPath if these terms are breached.

Consent

By registering, your organisation consents to these access limits and to BrightPath logging your organisation's access requests for audit purposes.
