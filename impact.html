ALIGNMENT

Supporting the Department of Basic Education's goals

BrightPath is designed as a tool that supports, rather than replaces, provincial and district processes. Here is how each part of the platform maps to broader DBE strategic priorities for basic and post-school education.

Improve learner performance & progression

By giving learners a reason to record achievement throughout Grades 11-12 - not only at matric - BrightPath encourages sustained engagement alongside academic work, supporting the province's continued NSC pass rate gains (85.01% in 2024, 86.1% in 2025).

Strengthen data quality & accountability

Every verification and approval action is logged against a named teacher or district official, giving districts an auditable record of who confirmed what and when - supporting DBE's data governance and accountability principles.

Expand access to post-school education & training

A single, district-approved portfolio reduces the information gap between rural learners and universities, colleges and bursary providers who would otherwise never see evidence of a learner's achievements beyond their marks.

Support youth employability

The downloadable, district-endorsed referral letter gives Grade 12 leavers a credible document to present directly to employers - responding to Limpopo's youth (15-34) unemployment rate of roughly 45.5%, well above the national average of 32.9%.
